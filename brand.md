# Brand — IntentLock

> **Product:** IntentLock — Private intentional browsing enforcer for Chrome.  
> **Core System:** Described in `DESIGN.md` and implemented across `newtab.css`, `intervention.css`, and `checkin-prompt.js`.

---

## 1. Brand Essence

- **One-liner:** Declare what you are doing. IntentLock watches your tabs and locks the page when you drift.
- **Tone:** Calm, precise, unyielding, private. Like an aircraft instrument panel or a mechanical shutter.
- **Demeanor:** Neither preachy nor patronizing. Never uses juvenile gamification, guilt-tripping, or corporate surveillance tropes.

---

## 2. Visual DNA

- **Aesthetic:** Swiss Modernism / Visualized Value Monochrome.
- **Canvas:** Cinema Black (`#000000`) or Stark White (`#ffffff`).
- **Typography:**
  - **Structure & Telemetry:** IBM Plex Mono.
  - **Intent & Humanity:** Source Serif 4.
- **Geometry:** 1px hairlines, 2px corner radius.
- **Texture:** Diagonal hatch line work (`.vv-hatch`).
- **Motion:** 160ms shutter-fast transitions with strict reduced-motion overrides.

---

## 3. Product Vocabulary

| Concept | Preferred Term | Forbidden / Discouraged Terms |
|---|---|---|
| Session start | **Lock in** / **Declare intent** | "Start timer", "Go", "Begin tracking" |
| The rule | **Intent** / **Vow** | "Goal", "Target", "Task item" |
| Off-task visit | **Drift** | "Distraction", "Violation", "Bad site" |
| Soft prompt | **Check-in** ("Still on task?") | "Warning", "Nudge", "Alert" |
| Full intervention | **Lock** | "Block page", "Paywall", "Banned" |
| Recovery action | **Reflect & continue** | "Snooze", "Bypass", "Cheat" |
